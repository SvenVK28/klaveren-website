# SCVK Media — Brand Assets and Usage

## Brand
**SCVK Media** is the preferred brand name. The initials represent the owner's names. The owner prefers this brand name over using only a first and last name.

## Domain
Planned domain: `scvkmedia.nl`. The owner previously saw it listed as available at MijnDomein, but availability and registration must be checked again. Do not assume it has been purchased.

## Logo
The owner has created a logo and wants it used as the standard brand mark. The concept includes a microphone integrated into the K. A black version is also desired or available. Use the actual approved asset rather than recreating it in code.

### Logo handling rules
- Ask the owner to attach the final logo file(s) to the website-building session.
- Prefer SVG; a transparent PNG is a suitable fallback.
- Preserve the logo's proportions, spacing, and details.
- Do not stretch, crop, skew, or add effects.
- Use the approved black-on-white version by default unless the supplied files specify otherwise.
- If a navy version is needed, obtain approval rather than applying an arbitrary filter.
- A text placeholder is acceptable during development only and must be easy to replace.

## Suggested colour palette
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Near-black: `#171A1D`
- Dark heading: `#101820`
- Muted text: `#626B73`
- Light surface: `#F5F7F8`
- Border: `#DDE2E6`
- Marine navy: `#12334A`
- Navy hover: `#0C2739`
- Optional petrol-blue accent: `#176B73`

White, near-black, and marine navy should dominate. Petrol blue is secondary.

## Typography
Use one legible, modern sans-serif typeface or a restrained pairing. Suggested options include Inter, Manrope, and Source Sans 3. Keep the hierarchy editorial and consistent.

## Brand voice
Professional, journalistic, direct, approachable, understated, and modern. Avoid a generic agency, lifestyle-influencer, or large production-company feel.

## Assets and details to confirm
- Final logo file(s).
- Approved portrait photograph.
- Approved portfolio thumbnails and video stills.
- LinkedIn URL and SCVK Media Instagram URL.
- Confirmed contact email address.
- Domain registration and hosting status.
- Any preferred or supplied font files.

Never invent contact details, social-media URLs, business details, or assets.
