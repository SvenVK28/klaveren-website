# SCVK Media — Website Copy Workflow

## Owner preference
The owner wants to write and approve the final website copy personally. AI-generated copy is not approved by default.

## Copy principles
- Use natural, concise Dutch that is easy to scan.
- Keep the tone professional and approachable.
- Prefer specific wording over generic marketing language.
- Be honest about the owner's status as a journalism student and his actual experience.
- Do not invent clients, awards, results, testimonials, or qualifications.
- Mark all proposed copy as a draft until it has been approved.

## Tone example
“Heb je een vraag of wil je een freelanceopdracht bespreken? Stuur gerust een bericht.”
English meaning: “Have a question or want to discuss a freelance assignment? Feel free to send a message.”
This is an example, not final approved copy.

## Approval workflow
1. Prepare drafts and label them clearly as drafts.
2. Let the owner review and edit the wording.
3. Mark copy as approved only after explicit confirmation.
4. Do not change approved wording without asking.
5. Before launch, check spelling, factual accuracy, links, and consistency.
