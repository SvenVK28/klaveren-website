# SCVK Media — Launch and Owner Checklist

## Before development
- [ ] Attach the approved homepage mock-up.
- [ ] Attach the approved About-page mock-up (“Over mij”).
- [ ] Attach the approved freelance-services mock-up.
- [ ] Attach the approved Contact-page mock-up.
- [ ] Attach the final logo file(s).
- [ ] Confirm whether `scvkmedia.nl` has been registered.
- [ ] Decide who controls the domain, hosting, and website accounts.
- [ ] Decide how the owner will edit content.
- [ ] Collect genuine portfolio projects and approved images.
- [ ] Confirm the LinkedIn and Instagram URLs.
- [ ] Confirm the contact email once it has been created.

## Content
- [ ] Homepage copy approved.
- [ ] About-page copy approved.
- [ ] Freelance services and descriptions approved.
- [ ] Contact-page copy approved.
- [ ] Project titles and summaries verified.
- [ ] Every work item has at least one relevant image.
- [ ] The owner's role is accurately described.
- [ ] Dates, publication details, and client claims verified.
- [ ] Publication, image, and video rights confirmed.
- [ ] No AI-generated placeholder is presented as genuine work.

## Design
- [ ] Consistent colour palette and typography.
- [ ] Consistent navigation across pages.
- [ ] Homepage shows portfolio work early.
- [ ] About page is concise.
- [ ] Freelance-services page is clear and does not overpromise.
- [ ] Contact page is uncluttered.
- [ ] Images are relevant and consistent.
- [ ] Mobile layout has been deliberately designed and tested.

## Functionality
- [ ] Portfolio filters work.
- [ ] Project pages work.
- [ ] Video embeds play responsively.
- [ ] Contact form successfully delivers a test message.
- [ ] Email alternative works, if used.
- [ ] LinkedIn and Instagram links are correct.
- [ ] Buttons and navigation work.
- [ ] No placeholder URLs remain.
- [ ] 404 page is useful.

## Owner editing and handover
- [ ] Owner can add a project without redesigning the website.
- [ ] Owner can replace a thumbnail.
- [ ] Owner can edit page and service copy.
- [ ] Owner can update email and social links.
- [ ] Owner understands whether changes require publishing or redeployment.
- [ ] Owner has access to domain, hosting, CMS, and relevant accounts.
- [ ] A short content-editing guide is provided.
- [ ] Backups and recovery are explained.
- [ ] Ongoing costs and renewal dates are documented.

## Legal, privacy, and SEO
- [ ] HTTPS works.
- [ ] Privacy information matches the actual form and tools.
- [ ] Cookie handling has been reviewed where relevant.
- [ ] Required business details are accurate, if applicable.
- [ ] Page titles and meta descriptions are present.
- [ ] Images have appropriate alt text.
- [ ] Sitemap and robots settings are correct where supported.
- [ ] Mobile usability and accessibility checks are complete.

## Final sign-off
Do not launch until the owner has reviewed the live preview, approved all public copy, tested the contact routes, and verified that the content-editing workflow works in practice.
