# SCVK Media — Visual Design References

## Purpose
Translate the owner's approved mock-ups into practical design guidance. Use them as inspiration rather than rigid templates that prevent useful improvements.

## Overall look and feel
- Editorial and journalistic, not generic software or agency branding.
- Modern, structured, calm, credible, and approachable.
- White background, near-black typography, and marine-navy accents.
- Clear grid, strong headlines, consistent alignment, and generous whitespace.
- Relevant photographs and video stills should make the work the focus.
- Professional and neutral rather than playful or overly decorative.

## What to inspect in each screenshot
- Header and logo placement.
- Navigation spacing and active-page indicator.
- Hero-section proportions and alignment.
- Image sizes, cropping, and relationship to text.
- Portfolio grid and card hierarchy.
- Section spacing and page rhythm.
- Typography scale and line lengths.
- Calls to action (CTAs).
- Footer structure.
- Mobile behaviour, if mobile references are supplied.

## Room for improvement
The owner wants the mock-ups used as a starting point, with room for more structured options. Improve clarity, navigation, mobile usability, accessibility, consistency, performance, and future content editing where appropriate. Do not replace the entire aesthetic without a clear reason. If proposing a major redesign, explain the benefit.

## Page direction

### Home
Show the work early instead of burying it below a long biography. A hero section, selected-work grid, short About preview, service preview, and contact CTA are reasonable. Every work item should have at least one relevant image. The full portfolio can be extensive while the homepage remains curated.

### Work (Dutch navigation label: “Mijn werk”)
Use a consistent editorial grid. Add filters if they improve browsing. Make each item distinguishable by category, title, summary, and thumbnail.

### About (Dutch navigation label: “Over mij”)
Keep the biography concise and easy to scan. Use a genuine portrait only if one has been supplied and approved. Avoid walls of text.

### Freelance Services (Dutch navigation label: “Freelance”)
Describe services plainly and honestly. Each service block can contain a short explanation, the Dutch price note **“Prijs in overleg”** (“Price to be discussed”), and a contact CTA labelled **“Neem contact op”** (“Get in touch”). Avoid presenting the owner as a large agency.

### Contact
Keep the form uncluttered. A form beside contact details can work on desktop; stack the sections on mobile. Never display invented contact details.

## Photography
The owner wants at least one image for every work or news item. This does not mean every page needs many unrelated images. Prioritise genuine project photographs, approved stills, and properly licensed material. Do not present AI-generated or stock imagery as documentary evidence of real reporting.

## Visual checks
Test at 320, 375, 430, 768, 1280, and 1440 pixels wide. Avoid horizontal scrolling, inconsistent spacing, poor crops, tiny text, and illegible logo usage.
